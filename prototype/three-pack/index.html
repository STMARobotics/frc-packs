Opening a pack, in WebGL

The real pack and card art, drawn with Three.js. Swipe across the top of the pack to tear it along the seam; the light coming out is the colour of the best card inside. Let go early and it closes again. Then tap through the cards: Rare and better arrive face down.

Three.js is the same 118 KB as the card prototype, so the card and the opening would share it. Speed is the same story: smooth on a mid-range phone profile, and the real costs are the download, GPU work and battery on low-end phones.

Compared with today's opening

  • Today's tear already follows your finger and rewinds. That stays the same here.
  • What WebGL adds: the strip really peels toward you in 3D, the rarity light spills out only where it's open, the cards rise out from inside the pack, and the flips have real depth with a light burst.
  • What it doesn't have yet: the sounds, the haptics, the pull summary and the softbody wobble of today's pack.
