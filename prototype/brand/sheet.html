FRC Packs brand marks

Five directions for the mark, each from the game's own world: packs, the single-seam rip, the constellation pack art, and Mythic teal as the payoff. None borrows from FIRST's marks. Each is shown as an app icon, at favicon sizes (32 and 16 px, bare and on the tile) and in the wordmark lockup, on the game's navy and on white.

1Seam (simplified, chosen)

A pack with its top lifted off along one teal seam, and a teal spark on the front. Four shapes: the light wedge is gone, so it stays crisp from 512 px down to a 16 px tab icon.

Chosen: Seam, simplified

The one clean tear is exactly what happens when you open a pack in the app, so the mark could only be FRC Packs. Simplified to four shapes, it stays clear at 16 px, works in one colour (the notification badge is the white silhouette with the spark cut out), and can animate: the lid can lift as a pack opens. It is now the favicon, the app icon and the header mark.
