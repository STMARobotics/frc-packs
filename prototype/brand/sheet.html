FRC Packs brand marks

Five directions for the mark, each from the game's own world: packs, the single-seam rip, the constellation pack art, and Mythic teal as the payoff. None borrows from FIRST's marks. Each is shown as an app icon, at favicon sizes (32 and 16 px, bare and on the tile) and in the wordmark lockup, on the game's navy and on white.

1Seam

A pack with its top torn off along one clean line, light spilling out. It is the game's signature moment, and the same single seam as the pack opening.

My pick: Seam

It is the only one that could only be FRC Packs: the one clean tear and the light coming out of the top are exactly what happens when you open a pack in the app. It stays a clear shape at 16 px (a block with a bright line and a lifted lid), it works in one colour, and it animates naturally: the lid can lift as a pack opens, or as a loading state. Runner-up: Spark Card, the simplest and the most legible, but it could belong to many card games.
